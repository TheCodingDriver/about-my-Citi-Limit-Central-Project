Limit Central – Market Risk Limits Management Platform

"At Citi, I worked on a project called Limit Central, which is a centralized platform for managing and monitoring market risk limits associated with Front Office trading activities.

To explain the business context, Front Office traders execute trades across different financial products, including derivatives. These trading activities generate market risk exposure, which needs to be monitored and controlled.

The Front Office trading systems provide information such as the trader, financial product, trading activity, and associated exposure. This information may come from upstream systems or market-data providers such as Bloomberg, depending on the workflow.

The primary purpose of Limit Central is to define, maintain, and manage the risk limits within which trading activities are permitted.

Market Risk Managers are key stakeholders. They assess the risk associated with trading activities using risk models and various risk factors. Based on the organization's risk appetite, they establish thresholds or limits for different trading activities, portfolios, or trading entities.

For example, if a particular trading activity has a defined exposure limit of $10 million, the actual risk exposure can be evaluated against that threshold.

If the exposure exceeds the permitted limit, it is considered a limit breach, which can trigger a workflow for investigation, review, escalation, or approval, depending on the business rules.

Limit Central helps centralize these limit definitions and their associated workflows, allowing Market Risk teams to manage risk consistently across Front Office activities.

From a technical perspective, I worked on the Java and Spring Boot backend, implementing business rules, validations, REST APIs, Spring Batch processing, and integrations with downstream applications through Kafka.

Overall, the application supports effective market risk governance by ensuring that trading exposures are evaluated against defined risk thresholds and that limit-related exceptions are handled through controlled workflows."
